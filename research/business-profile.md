# DongWorkx: business profile

## Who they are
DongWorkx is a motorcycle repair centre and motor parts shop run by **Brando Ramones** [brief, S11]. It has two branches in Central Luzon [S4, S5]:

- **Gerona, Tarlac:** Mac Arthur Highway, Purok Ilang Ilang, Brgy. Magaspac. This is on the main north–south highway, so riders passing through can stop in.
- **Guimba, Nueva Ecija:** Provincial Road, Saranay District, Guimba 3115.

Their Facebook page dates from January 2017 [S8], so the business has had an online presence for close to ten years. We don't yet know when the shop itself opened, so we won't claim a year.

## What they sell
- **Repair and service** for motorcycles [S1].
- **Motor parts** ("Motorparts Shop" on the logo) [S10].
- **Fuel-injection specialist** ("FI Specialist" on the logo) [S10]. This is their main difference from other shops. Most new scooters and underbones sold in the Philippines (Honda Click, Yamaha Mio/NMAX/Aerox, Suzuki Raider FI and similar) are fuel injected, and many small shops can't diagnose FI faults properly. Note: we have **not verified** a service list, the brands they work on, scanner/tools, or prices. Everything in that area is sample content until the owner confirms.
- The logo art shows performance parts (injectors, a big-bore rotor, CVT pulley, shock, exhaust, brake caliper). This suggests a performance/upgrade side as well as basic repair. Not verified.

## Who buys
Local riders in Gerona, Guimba and nearby towns. Most likely motorcycle commuters, delivery and habal-habal/tricycle riders whose bike is their income, plus enthusiasts modifying scooters. When they arrive, they usually have a bike that won't start or runs rough, they need it fixed fast and at a fair price, and they want to know the price before work starts. Enthusiasts arrive excited and want parts and upgrades.

## Voice
On Facebook, the page uses casual Taglish captions and reels with hashtags (#DongworkX) [S9]. The brand art is loud and racing-sticker styled. The website should sound confident, local and friendly: simple English with a little Tagalog where it feels natural. It should not sound corporate.

## Current web presence and gaps (the case for upgrading)
- **No website.** Facebook is their only online presence. The page's "Website" field just links to the owner's personal profile [S11].
- **Not on Google Maps** (none found) [S12]. Someone searching "motorcycle repair Gerona" or "FI repair Guimba" can't find them. Competitors like Guanzon and Premio appear in store locators [competitors].
- **Hours say "Always open"** [S6]. This is probably wrong, and it erodes trust when a customer drives over to a closed shop.
- **No booking.** Customers have to message on Messenger, wait for a reply, and arrange a slot by chat.
- **No parts catalogue.** Customers can't check if a part is in stock or what it costs without messaging.
- **They pay for ads that send people to a Facebook page.** A site with booking and parts would give that ad spend a place to convert.
- One public phone number for two branches, and we can't tell which branch it's for.
